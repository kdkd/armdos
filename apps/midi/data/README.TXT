C:\MIDI - General MIDI songs for the ARM-PC's MPU-401 synthesizer

Play them with PLAYMIDI (C:\DOS\PLAYMIDI.EXE):

    PLAYMIDI C:\MIDI\*.MID          all of them, one after the other
    PLAYMIDI /L C:\MIDI\BACH846     one, over and over (Esc stops)

BACH846  MID  J.S. Bach: Prelude in C major, BWV 846 (piano)
TOCCATA  MID  J.S. Bach: Toccata and Fugue in D minor, BWV 565 (organ)
BRANDBG2 MID  J.S. Bach: Brandenburg Concerto No. 2, I. Allegro (ensemble)
NACHTMUS MID  W.A. Mozart: Eine kleine Nachtmusik, I. Allegro (strings)
FURELISE MID  L. van Beethoven: Fur Elise, WoO 59 (piano)
MTNKING  MID  E. Grieg: In the Hall of the Mountain King (piano)
ENTERTNR MID  S. Joplin: The Entertainer (piano)
MAPLELF  MID  S. Joplin: Maple Leaf Rag (piano)

All eight were typeset with GNU LilyPond by volunteers of the Mutopia
Project (www.mutopiaproject.org) and placed in the public domain by their
typesetters: free to distribute, modify and perform. Only the song titles
inside the files were added (and a tempo where the score had none).
