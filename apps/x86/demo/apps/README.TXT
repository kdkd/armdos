ELBOW GALLERY - real IBM PC programs, run unmodified on the ARM PC
===================================================================

Every program here is a genuine x86 DOS binary (1980s to 2000s, most of
them from the 1980s), copied byte for byte from its publisher's release.  None
was recompiled or patched for the ARM PC: ELBOW runs the original Intel
machine code.  (One exception, on purpose: GW-BASIC was assembled here, on
the ARM, from Microsoft's source - see below.)  Type MENU for the games,
OFFICE for the business programs.

Each title is here because its rights holder released it for free
distribution: freeware by the rights holder's own statement, or an open
licence (MIT, GPL).  Each program's own licence or release notes are
next to it, unmodified.  The original archives and their SHA-256 hashes
are listed below.  SHA256.TXT has the hash of every file in this
directory.

Old notices inside the games ("shareware", "$10 registration", "may not
be distributed by BBS...", the 4206 Mayflower Dr. address) are history.
The later freeware releases below supersede them.  The Apogee titles
are freeware, NOT public domain: Apogee keeps the copyright and does
not allow them to be sold or used in other projects.

GW-BASIC (BASIC\)  - built on this machine
  Microsoft GW-BASIC, (c) 1982-1983 Microsoft Corporation, released as
  source code under the MIT licence on 21 May 2020
  (https://github.com/microsoft/GW-BASIC).  That release lacks the
  machine-specific "OEM" modules; D. Spinellis, S. Gros and T.K. Chia wrote
  them (https://codeberg.org/tkchia/GW-BASIC, commit ccde2b96, same MIT
  licence - see BASIC\SOURCE\LICENSE.TXT; the program's banner names them).
  BASIC\GWBASIC.EXE was assembled FROM THAT SOURCE ON THE ARM PC: Microsoft
  MASM 5.10 and LINK 3.65 (x86 programs, from Microsoft's MIT-licensed
  MS-DOS 4.00 source release, https://github.com/microsoft/MS-DOS,
  v4.0/src/TOOLS; licence in BASIC\SOURCE\MSDOS4.LIC) ran under ELBOW and
  assembled all 38 modules.  BASIC\SOURCE\BUILD.BAT does it again.
  SHA-256 of that GWBASIC.EXE:
           005247d5bef8e7a622b001aa9ce1a75c5d854792a11f2a29dfcb6e24d28d5774
  MASM.EXE 1c6286c69b616160b8475ad17453ee872702a2c98075c2159ec792a1c745275f
  LINK.EXE 124a3c800edc16b60f696d35a9dfec798b68b78fe2ca90c5988ea76bdeab1a8a
  Samples: LOAN.BAS (loan table, can LPRINT it), SPIRAL.BAS (CGA).
  SYSTEM leaves GW-BASIC.

  The Spreadsheet Calculator: public domain (James Gosling's vc, Mark
  Weiser, Jeff Buhrt et al.; see SC\README), ported to DOS and OS/2 in

  Samples: BUDGET.SC, TUTORIAL.SC.
  From:    the Simtel MS-DOS archive, msdos/spredsht/sc621_3.zip ("Spread-
           sheet Calculator sc v6.21, OS/2 and DOS"), from the Internet
           Archive's copy of the CCT Simtel CD-ROM, October 1994.
  SHA-256: c0e5c3bca19834aca3287a063d025be329878291067537622e40241abb12ad72
  (sc32.exe, the 32-bit emx build, is left out: it is not a real-mode
  program.  Two source files are renamed to fit 8.3 names.)
