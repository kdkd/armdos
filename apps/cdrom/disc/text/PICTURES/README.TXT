PICTURES
════════

Four public-domain NASA photographs as GIF files (GIF89a), 320 x 200 in
256 colours - the size of VGA mode 13h, so they fill the screen of a
graphics viewer. Use your favourite GIF viewer program.

  EARTHRSE.GIF  Earthrise, Apollo 8, 1968
  BLUEMARB.GIF  The Blue Marble, Apollo 17, 1972
  ALDRIN.GIF    Edwin Aldrin on the Moon, Apollo 11, 1969
  MOON.GIF      The Moon seen by the Galileo probe, December 1992
