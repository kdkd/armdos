@X0F  SFARM - THE SOUTH FLORIDA ARM USERS' GROUP@X07

  Second Tuesday of every month, 7:30 PM, in the back room of Sal's Pizza
  on Hollywood Blvd.  Bring your machine, your questions, and five dollars
  for the pie fund.  Newcomers welcome.  Nobody will make you give a talk
  (Maria might).

@X0EEvery meeting@X07
  * Disk of the Month: a 5 1/4" floppy of the best freeware and shareware
    we found, one dollar, or free if you bring a blank.  Also in the Files
    section here, for those who prefer to stay home in their pajamas.
  * Show and Tell: somebody always brings something.  Last month it was
    Harold's model railroad signal controller, run by an ARM/AT.  The
    trains ran on time.  The sysop took notes.
  * The Help Table: bring a sick computer, leave with a well one (usually).
    Karen Whitfield has fixed four power supplies and one marriage there.

@X0EThe Barrel Shifter@X07
  Our newsletter, edited by Maria Delgado.  Four pages, photocopied, more
  opinions than a Flame Pit thread.  Past issues had "Your First Batch
  File", "What the TURBO Button Really Does" (Karen), "A Beginner's Guide
  to Getting Eaten by a Grue" (Lenny Szabo) and the sysop's column,
  "Notes from the Spare Bedroom".

@X0EComing up@X07
  * Susan Oyelaran: "ARM Assembly in 30 Minutes (or Your Pizza is Free)"
  * Bill Tran, by speakerphone from California: "9600 Baud and Beyond"
  * Gordon Kessler of The 386 Fortress has been invited to present "Why
    the 386 Wins".  He has accepted.  Please be kind.  Please bring
    earplugs.

  Officers: Maria Delgado (newsletter, and the one who actually organizes
  everything), Karen Whitfield (hardware), Harold Pruitt (treasurer, since
  he's the only one who balances a checkbook), Europa (whatever's left).
