                     ARM-DOS 4.00  -  Things to try
                     ==============================

  This PC's processor is an ARM926EJ-S. Every program here is ARM code,
  except the x86 ones, which the ELBOW box translates as they run.

  THE CLASSICS
    DIR, VER, MEM /PROGRAM     how DOS 4 sees this machine
    CHKDSK                     check the 128 MB hard disk
    TYPE D:\README.TXT         drive D: is yours: what you save there stays
                               through every update of ARM-DOS
    DOSSHELL                   the DOS 4.00 Shell, menus and all
    TREE C:\ /F | MORE         pipes and filters work as they should
    AC                         ARM Commander, a two-panel file manager
    GEM                        Digital Research's GEM desktop, native on
                               ARM (GEM /V for 640x480 colour)

  PEEK UNDER THE HOOD
    ARMINFO                    system information and benchmarks
                               (then press the TURBO button and re-test)
    TURBO MAX                  lift the clock limit: as fast as your computer
                               can go (TURBO RESTORE puts it back)
    DEBUG C:\COMMAND.COM       then type U to disassemble its ARM code
    FILE C:\DOS\*.EXE          what kind of files are these?
    CRASH                      a guided tour of ARM exceptions

  RUN x86 PROGRAMS             watch the X86 lamp on the front panel
    CD \ELBOW, then DEMO       genuine 8086 programs, incl. MS-DOS 2.0's MASM
    X86GAMES                   real x86 classics (Kroz, ZZT, Sopwith, Apogee's
                               platformers...), translated to ARM as they run,
                               and Future Crew's Second Reality demo (#17)
    X86APPS                    real x86 office software: GW-BASIC (assembled
                               on this ARM from Microsoft's source!), VDE,
                               SC spreadsheet, DataPerfect, MS-DOS Kermit

  GAMES                        (in C:\GAMES; plug in a gamepad and run
                               JOYTEST to use it as a PC joystick)
    DOOM, WOLF3D, QUAKE,       CD into their directories first
    DUKE3D
    ZORK1, ZORK2, ZORK3        in C:\GAMES\ZORK
    ADVENT                     Colossal Cave, in C:\GAMES\ADVENT
    GETKEEN                    in C:\GAMES\KEEN: fetches Keen Dreams
                               from The ARM Pit BBS by modem
    HEXEN2                     bring your own demo data: read C:\GAMES\HEXEN2

  MAKE THINGS
    EDIT HELLO.C               a full-screen editor
    TC                         ARM Turbo C: an IDE with a C compiler that
                               runs on this PC (F9 builds, Ctrl+F9 runs)
    QB                         ARM QuickBASIC: try QB /RUN C:\QB\BANANAS
    PAINT                      ARM Paint - and print on the dot-matrix
    BANNER                     print a giant banner across the paper

  DOCTOR                       Dr. ARMitso, the talking psychologist
  SAY Hello there              the Sound Blaster talks back

  MUSIC                        (put the Sampler disc in the CD-ROM drive)
    CDPLAY                     play CD audio from drive D:
    PLAYMIDI C:\MIDI\*.MID     General MIDI on the sound card
    C:\DEMO\DEMO               a 1990s-style demo, all ARM assembly

  GO ONLINE                    (set the modem speed on its front panel)
    ONLINE                     ARM-DOS Online: live encyclopedia, weather,
                               news and pictures over the modem
    TERM                       dial The ARM Pit BBS at 555-1989
                               (its file areas hold extra tools you won't
                               find on this disk - worth a look)

  INTERNATIONAL                DOS 4 style keyboards, code pages, countries
    KEYB GR                    a German keyboard: Y and Z swap, AltGr+Q = @,
                               the key right of á then E = ‚; KEYB alone
                               shows the layout. Also FR UK SP IT SG SF NL
                               BE DK NO SV SU PO CF LA - and DV, Dvorak.
                               Ctrl+Alt+F1 = US, Ctrl+Alt+F2 = back again.
                               The PC KEYS on the page follow.
    In CONFIG.SYS:  DEVICE=C:\DOS\DISPLAY.SYS CON=(EGA,437,1)
    MODE CON CP PREP=((850) C:\DOS\EGA.CPI)
    MODE CON CP SEL=850        new characters on the screen (code page 850)
    NLSFUNC, then CHCP 850     the whole system switches code page
    In CONFIG.SYS:  COUNTRY=049
                               DATE, TIME and DIR the German way

  POPUP                        a pop-up utility: press Ctrl+Alt+P anytime

  ABOUT THIS PC                what it is, whom to call, and who made it
